Your shift starts here

Small details.
Satisfying service.

01 / COOK

Find your rhythm

Grill beef patties, build burgers, fry potatoes and pour cola. Keep several meals moving while guests wait.

02 / CUSTOMIZE

Make it their way

No cheese, no veggies, extra ketchup or salad dressing: each burger has its own request. Match the right meal to the right guest.

03 / GROW

Work your way up

Meet service and rating goals to move from Crew Member to Shift Lead. Higher roles bring higher pay, with food profit and your wages shown separately.

Made to order

Two burgers.
Two different tastes.

Read each burger's changes, choose its toppings and sauce, then wrap and serve. Different burgers in one order can be served in either order, as long as each request is met.

Illustrated tutorials cover the basics, special requests and your first night after work.

Clock out. Settle in.

The day doesn't end
at the counter.

Bring home a staff meal or order a dinner surprise. Take a shower, get some rest and keep an eye on your energy for the next shift.

Start in a Cozy Studio, discover the City Apartment, and work toward a Garden Cottage or Riverside Loft. Your in-game phone lets you switch between homes you own.

Game information & support

Sizzle & Settle is an English-language, landscape cooking and life simulation game for iPhone and iPad.

Public support contact and App Store link will be added before release. This local information page is not yet a live App Store support destination.

All guests, reviews, phone services, housing purchases and wages are fictional. Game coins have no cash value. No real food ordering, employment, property transactions or external review posting is offered.
