Privacy policy · Effective October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how junhao hong (the developer) handles information in Sizzle & Settle for iPhone and iPad.

Advertising is disabled in the current release configuration. No third-party advertising or analytics SDK is bundled, and no advertising identifier is read or transmitted by this build. The game has no account registration.

1. Information handled by the game

The game uses app-specific storage on your device for limited preferences, including whether the introductory tutorial has been completed. Gameplay state such as orders, wages, housing and mood is processed on your device. This does not mean that every part of a play session is saved between launches. No developer-operated server receives this information.

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library. The phone, map, messages, photos, delivery services and restaurant reviews inside the game are fictional interfaces, not access to your actual device data or external services.

2. Advertising, tracking and purchases

The game includes a disabled integration framework for possible future advertising. Before an advertising-enabled release, the developer must identify the advertising partners, update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures, and configure the service. An active advertising integration will request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency system before using tracking-dependent advertising. You can refuse permission and continue playing. The current conservative integration does not initialize advertising SDKs or request ads when permission is denied, restricted or undecided.

When advertising is enabled and permission is granted, the configuration interface sends only the app's bundle identifier, version, build number, platform and build environment to its configured HTTPS server. This request contains no advertising identifier, vendor identifier or fingerprint. As with any network request, the receiving server can see the connection's IP address. No such configuration requests are sent with the current disabled, unconfigured release settings. Advertising partners' own data practices must be disclosed before any SDK is activated; granting ATT permission alone does not override other required privacy choices.

No in-app purchases are implemented. Coins, wages, housing and meals are game mechanics with no cash value. Fictional review campaigns do not publish reviews to the App Store or to real businesses.

3. Sharing and platform services

We do not sell, rent or share personal information collected through the game because we do not collect it. Apple may process information independently when you download the game, use operating-system services, enable device backups or choose to share diagnostics with Apple. Those activities are governed by your settings and Apple's privacy policy, not by a developer-operated collection service in this game.

4. Retention and your choices

Local preferences remain in the app's storage until removed by the operating system or by deleting the app. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, generally removes its local container. Device or iCloud backups may retain or restore information according to your Apple settings. There is no developer account to delete and no server-side gameplay profile to request from us.

5. Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or require users to enter personal details. If you believe personal information has been sent to the developer outside the game, please use the privacy contact below. This statement does not replace the age rating shown on the App Store.

6. This website and voluntary contact

These static pages contain no developer analytics scripts, advertising tags, cookies or contact forms. Once hosted, the hosting provider may process basic technical request information, such as an IP address, to deliver and secure the pages. Hosting-specific details will be added when a provider is selected. If you voluntarily contact the developer, the information you choose to provide may be used to respond to your request; please do not send sensitive information.

7. Changes

If the game's features or data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised. Any required disclosures or permissions will be addressed before the new practices are introduced.
